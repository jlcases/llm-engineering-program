# AWS Certified AI Practitioner — AIF-C01

Preparation material organized according to the five domains of the AIF-C01 exam. Use this alongside the [official exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-practitioner-01/ai-practitioner-01.html) and AWS Skill Builder; the official guide is the source of truth regarding weightings and scope.

Coverage verified on **August 21, 2026** against revision **1.1** of the guide, published
on April 30, 2026. Includes agentic AI, Amazon Quick, Kiro, Strands Agents, Amazon Bedrock
AgentCore, model distillation, and current changes to services in scope.

## How to interpret the result

The percentage score on the repo's practice exam does not directly predict the scaled score of the actual exam.
As an internal preparation benchmark, aim for at least an 80% overall score and no domain below
70% across two separate passes. Pay special attention to questions where you answered correctly
with low confidence: these are fragile wins.

The practice questions in this repository are original and are not leaked questions from the
actual exam.
